About Kokopo Stay

A family-run hotel with over a decade of hosting travellers on the East New Britain coast.

Home / About & Reviews
Since 2013

Built by a local family, for travellers who want more than a room

Kokopo Stay started as a six-room guesthouse run by the Namaliu family and has grown into an 18-room boutique hotel — without losing the personal touch that guests keep coming back for.

Today the team is still mostly local, many trained in-house, and still greets returning guests by name. We built this hotel around the coastline we grew up on, and we like sharing it.
